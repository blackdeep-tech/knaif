---
title: Privacy policy
description: What knaif.dev collects about visitors — no cookies, no analytics, no trackers.
---

knaif.dev is run by Blackdeep Technologies Ltd. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or
trackers.

- Like any website, it is served by a hosting provider that keeps standard access logs: IP
  address, browser, page requested and time.
- If you choose a light or dark theme, your browser remembers it locally. It is never sent to us.
- Links to GitHub and Hugging Face take you to their sites, where their own privacy
  policies apply.

Questions: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/blackdeep-tech/knaif/issues).
